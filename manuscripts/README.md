# Manuscripts

- `thesis/`: architecture chapter drafts, authoring context, and the encrypted thesis-angle note.
- `jamia/drafts/`: local revision snapshots moved from `work/`; ignored by Git.
- `jamia/scripts/`: local historical revision scripts; ignored by Git.

Revision scripts retain their original contents and paths as a record of past
editing sessions. They are not maintained commands and should not be executed as
part of setup or verification. Plaintext thesis-angle notes remain ignored.
Engineering documentation belongs in `docs/`.
